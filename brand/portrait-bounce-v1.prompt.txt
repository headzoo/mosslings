Use case: one character painting, warped in code into a bounce. Style reference: public/mosslings/welcome-woodland-varied.webp. Gentle watercolor/gouache moss ball, tiny black eyes, tiny feet, fuzzy texture, one solid body. Grayscale only so the portrait renderer can lay genetic color over it. Soft painted fringe, transparent background.

The runtime strip is not 15 separate drawings. One master pose is scaled and lifted through a hop so the face stays attached:
settle, squash, launch, peak, fall, land, back to settle, sampled at 15 even steps.
Width and height stay inverse. The feet stay planted on the squash and the landing. The body lifts on the way up.

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, then soft-matted and LANCZOS warped.
Runtime asset: public/mosslings/portrait-bounce-v1.png (256×3840 vertical strip, one 256×256 cell per frame).
Neutral grayscale shading and the soft silhouette are retained; individual colors and patterns are applied per frame.
