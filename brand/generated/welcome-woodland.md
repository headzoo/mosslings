## Prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: artwork for the Mosslings game's welcome splash. Reference image: attached mosslings.png defines character identity and painterly storybook style. Create a new landscape 3:2 illustration of a little gathering of these round fuzzy moss creatures on a moss-covered forest log: a green sprout-topped creature, a cream-patched creature, a daisy-topped creature and a small blue-green creature. Preserve their tiny black eyes, tiny feet, understated sweet expressions and detailed fuzzy botanical texture. Ferns, little flowers, soft woodland light and distant forgotten stone ruins. Warm cream, olive and forest-green palette, gentle watercolor/gouache storybook finish like the reference. Full scene, no panels, no text, no lettering, no logo, no watermark. These are living tufts of moss, not furry mammals.
