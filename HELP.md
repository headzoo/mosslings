# How to play

![Help](/help/buttons/help.png) opens this page.

You play God. The Mosslings do not take orders. You shape the land, and they live in what you make.

Their differences come from real genetics. A child inherits a blend of its parents' traits, with a little of its own, so families differ in courage, speed, and how much they need to eat. Click a Mossling to see those qualities.

To keep them alive, make farmland. ![Grow](/help/buttons/grow.png) plows fields and plants crops. ![Rain](/help/buttons/rain.png) keeps those crops wet. A crop ripens over wet months and withers if the ground dries out. Showers fall on their own, but they are not enough.

Food along the top is ripe crop tiles. About one ripe tile feeds one Mossling. Each month that the fields fall short, health drops, and the hungriest lose the most. Stay short and Mosslings starve and die. Enough ripe food lets health climb back.

There is still no score. Watch, change one thing, and see who copes.

## Moving around

![The map and zoom buttons](/help/screens/map.png)

- Drag the map to look around.
- ![Zoom out](/help/buttons/zoom-out.png) ![Zoom in](/help/buttons/zoom-in.png) Scroll to zoom in and out, or use these buttons on the map.
- Click a tile to see what is there.
- Press Esc to put down the power you have selected.

## Time

![The year and time controls](/help/screens/time.png)

The buttons beside Help change how the year passes.

- ![Mute](/help/buttons/mute.png) turns the music off or on.
- ![Pause](/help/buttons/pause.png) holds the season where it is. Press it again to play at normal speed.
- ![Skip](/help/buttons/skip.png) jumps ahead to the next spring.
- ![Fast forward](/help/buttons/fast.png) speeds the year up. Keep pressing it to go faster, until it returns to normal speed.

The heart beside the date beats with each passing month.

## Powers

Choose a power on the left, then click a tile. Some powers only work on certain land.

- ![Rain](/help/buttons/rain.png) wets the ground, feeds the soil, and can put out a fire. Crops need it to grow. A long rain in one place can flood it.
- ![Grow](/help/buttons/grow.png) plows fields and grows crops.
- ![Ground](/help/buttons/ground.png) lays down a patch of dirt.
- ![Raze](/help/buttons/raze.png) clears forest back to dirt. Click a tree.
- ![Disease](/help/buttons/disease.png) sickens nearby Mosslings.
- ![Fire](/help/buttons/fire.png) spreads and burns the land down to dirt.
- ![Tornado](/help/buttons/tornado.png) wanders across the map, tearing up crops and anyone in its path.
- ![Quake](/help/buttons/quake.png) cracks the ground across a wide area.
- ![Lightning](/help/buttons/lightning.png) strikes at once and scorches the spot you chose.
- ![Meteor](/help/buttons/meteor.png) falls and leaves a crater.

Mosslings will try to get out of the way of a disaster.

## The world

![Resource counters](/help/screens/resources.png)

The counters along the top track Mosslings, food, wood, stone, and water. Seasons turn on their own. Showers, fires, tornadoes, quakes, lightning, and meteors also arrive on their own. Rain favors the fields, but it is not enough to keep the crops watered. If too much rain sits on one place, or a rare storm rolls in, the ground floods. Flooded land, stone, and trees become water. Watch a while, then change one thing and see who copes.
