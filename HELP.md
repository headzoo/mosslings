# How to play

![Help](/help/buttons/help.png) opens this page.

You play God. The Mosslings do not take orders. You shape the land, and they live in what you make.

Their differences come from real genetics. A child inherits a blend of its parents' traits, with a little of its own, so families differ in courage, speed, and how much they need to eat. Click a Mossling to see those qualities.

To keep them alive, make farmland. ![Crops](/help/buttons/crops.png) turns ground and forest into crop land. ![Rain](/help/buttons/rain.png) keeps those crops wet, and ![Sun](/help/buttons/sun.png) gives them light. A crop ripens over six months that are both wet and lit, and withers if the ground dries out. Showers fall on their own, on the fields and on the trees, grass, and moss, but they are not enough to keep that wild growth green. Away from water it dries and turns brown. There is no sunlight unless you bring it.

Food along the top is ripe crop tiles. About one ripe tile feeds one Mossling. Each month that the fields fall short, health drops, and the hungriest lose the most. Stay short and Mosslings starve and die. Enough ripe food lets health climb back.

Winter stops the fields. Crops stay where they are, but they do not grow, wither, or count as food until spring. Health falls each hungry month, and a Mossling at full health can last the winter. One who goes in already weak may not. Autumn still feeds them, so that is the season to fill health back up. The land shows the turn: leaves go amber, then the trees stand bare, the grass thins, and snow drifts across the high ground, and a few of those drifts bury the grass, moss, dirt, and trees in solid white. It thins out within a few tiles of water. Late autumn starts to freeze the water a white-blue, the ice holds through winter, and it melts through spring. Winter clouds drop snow. It still wets the soil.

There is still no score. Watch, change one thing, and see who copes.

## Moving around

![The map and zoom buttons](/help/screens/map.png)

- ![Zoom out](/help/buttons/zoom-out.png) ![Zoom in](/help/buttons/zoom-in.png) Scroll to zoom in and out, or use these buttons on the map. Zoom out to see the whole world.
- When you are zoomed in, a white box on the little map shows where you are looking. Drag that box to move the view. Click somewhere else on the little map to jump there.
- Click a tile to see what is there.
- Press Esc to put down the power you have selected.

## Time

![The year and time controls](/help/screens/time.png)

The buttons beside Help change how the year passes.

- ![Mute](/help/buttons/mute.png) turns the music off or on.
- ![Pause](/help/buttons/pause.png) holds the season where it is. Press it again to play at normal speed.
- ![Skip](/help/buttons/skip.png) jumps ahead to the next spring.
- ![Fast forward](/help/buttons/fast.png) speeds the year up. Keep pressing it to go faster, until it returns to normal speed.

The heart beside the date beats with each passing month.

## Powers

Choose a power on the left, then click a tile. Hold and drag to paint that power across every tile you touch. Some powers only work on certain land.

- ![Rain](/help/buttons/rain.png) wets the ground, feeds the soil, and can put out a fire. Crops need it to grow. Trees, grass, and moss need it too, unless they stand within four tiles of water. Without it they turn brown, and fire spreads farther through that dry growth. A long rain in one place can flood it. Lightning strikes under the cloud about one second in ten.
- ![Sun](/help/buttons/sun.png) lights the ground and comforts nearby Mosslings. It can lift the black death and restore some health. The dead stay dead. Crops ripen only in months that are both wet and lit.
- ![Crops](/help/buttons/crops.png) turns ground and forest into crop land. Click grass, dirt, or a tree. Crops ripen over six months of rain and sun.
- ![Disease](/help/buttons/disease.png) sickens nearby Mosslings.
- ![Fire](/help/buttons/fire.png) spreads and burns the land down to dirt. Dry brown trees, grass, and moss carry the flames farther than wet ground.
- ![Tornado](/help/buttons/tornado.png) wanders across the map, tearing up crops and anyone in its path.
- ![Quake](/help/buttons/quake.png) cracks the ground across a wide area.
- ![Lightning](/help/buttons/lightning.png) strikes at once and scorches the spot you chose.
- ![Meteor](/help/buttons/meteor.png) falls and leaves a crater.

Mosslings will try to get out of the way of a disaster.

## The world

![Resource counters](/help/screens/resources.png)

The counters along the top track Mosslings, food, trees, stone, and water. Seasons turn on their own. Spring fills the trees and meadows back in. Summer is fully green. Bees and butterflies drift around the green tiles in those two seasons and vanish in autumn. Autumn turns the leaves, and winter leaves the trees bare, the grass thin, and patchy snow on the high ground. Shores stay clearer. Food is zero all winter. Showers, fires, tornadoes, quakes, lightning, and meteors also arrive on their own. Showers fall on crops, trees, grass, and moss. In winter they fall as snow, and they still wet the ground. They are still not enough to keep inland growth watered. Within four tiles of water, trees, grass, and moss stay green in the growing seasons. Farther off they dry and turn brown, and fire runs farther through that dry land. If too much rain sits on one place, or a rare storm rolls in, the ground floods. Flooded land, stone, and trees become water. Watch a while, then change one thing and see who copes.
